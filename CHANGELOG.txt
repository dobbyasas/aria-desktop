ARIA FOR MAC - VERSION HISTORY
==============================

Version 1.20.0
Created: 2026-09-05

Added:
- Added sample-accurate gapless album playback on Mac, preparing the remaining album before audio begins and scheduling tracks on one continuous output clock.
- Added album preparation progress and ahead-of-time buffering for mixed queues.

Fixed:
- Removed per-track player recreation and decoder startup delays at album boundaries.
- Preserved native sample rates and valid audio frames, including AAC and MP3 padding metadata, while keeping seek, repeat, queue edits, remote controls, and the visualizer connected to the new engine.

Version 1.19.0
Created: 2026-09-05

Added:
- Added a vinyl Mac player with a spinning artwork label, a queue that curves around the record, and playback controls beneath the queue.
- Added a compact lyrics sleeve under the vinyl with an expanded reading view.

Changed:
- The record preserves its angle when paused and respects Reduce Motion; queue and lyric scrolling follow the current track and line.

Version 1.18.0
Created: 2026-09-05

Added:
- Aria devices now join one shared playback session automatically, keeping audio on a single host while other devices show and control its live track, queue, progress, volume, shuffle, and repeat state.
- Added a playback-session menu for starting independent playback on this Mac or rejoining the shared session.

Version 1.17.0
Created: 2026-09-05

Added:
- Added title, artist, and year sorting to the album collection.

Changed:
- Redesigned album cards and album detail pages with vinyl treatments, adaptive hero layouts, clearer metadata, and streamlined playback actions.
